# Plant reference photographs

These photographs illustrate species; they do not certify the authored 3D concepts or guarantee cultivar colour. Botanical facts come from the separate Hong Kong plant guides. Source-file identities and licenses were checked on 11 October 2026 and 17 October 2026. The original twelve retain their Wikimedia-provided JPEGs; the eighteen additions use Wikimedia-provided thumbnails resized and converted to JPEG. The app crops thumbnails with CSS and shows the full image in detail.
