# Plant model assets

Bundled assets from Poly Haven, licensed [CC0-1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/):

- [Shrub 03](https://polyhaven.com/a/shrub_03), modeled by Rico Cilliers. Four narrow-stem variants, 8,287 source triangles in total.
- [Fern 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/fern_02), modeled by Rico Cilliers and scanned by Rob Tuytel. Four leaf-cluster variants, 6,232 source triangles in total.

Poly Haven permits modification, commercial use and redistribution: https://polyhaven.com/license. Attribution is appreciated but not required.

The GLBs package the official 1K glTF, binary geometry and three unchanged JPEG textures for each asset into self-contained files. Variants are centered at the base and uniformly scaled at runtime. Download URLs, official-file SHA-256 values, packaging notes and bundled GLB hashes are recorded in `manifest.json`. Geometry and textures are not generated by this project.

Landscape roofs and courtyards combine low fern clusters with taller stems. Light-touch courtyards and terrace planters also use ferns. Extensive roofs, facade vegetation and courtyard trees retain procedural geometry. View → Plant appearance switches between textured assets and simplified planting. Asset loading is independent of IFC import; either asset can fall back without blocking the workspace.

These are visual assets, not horticultural recommendations for Hong Kong or engineering assemblies. Decorative instances do not change coverage, cost or carbon quantities. The Sketchfab Ivy asset was not downloaded or redistributed because its download endpoint required authentication.
