# Roof form and default planting

App policy updated 14 October 2026; source review 11 October 2026. These sources explain possible roof systems; they do not establish suitability of our demonstration buildings.

| Roof form | Planning approach | Current app behaviour |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Near-flat roof / flat podium | Continuous extensive planting beds; deeper gardens only with reviewed space and construction | Conditional candidate when roof identity and upward geometry pass screening |
| Mono-pitch / gable / butterfly / saw-tooth | Sloped systems can require retention, erosion control and different planting methods | Conditional concept candidate; unresolved slope/system checks disclosed |
| Barrel / vaulted / curved / dome | Bespoke systems exist; varying slopes need project-specific design | Conditional concept candidate; unresolved slope/system checks disclosed |
| Unknown or complex mesh | Check geometry, roof identity and construction first | No assumed approval |

The former ≤5° roof eligibility cutoff and 20° roof-face clipping cutoff have been removed at the user’s request. Roof slope/form is retained for explanation, not approval. Original upward geometry, roof/slab identity and top-envelope checks still determine candidates. Adjacent source parts are grouped to describe roof form, using a conservative shared-vertex heuristic rather than a confirmed IFC assembly relation. Mesh normal grouping is an estimated shape descriptor, not a precise architectural classification. IFC can lack structural capacity, waterproofing, rooting depth, irrigation, roof plant/equipment, usable setbacks and access details.

Continuous coverage is clipped to original display faces/openings and to the confirmed usable area. It does not invent clear walkways or automatically identify/avoid every obstacle. A user-confirmed footprint and an engineering review remain necessary. Sloped/curved concept previews do not supply a construction-ready roof system: retention, anti-slip/erosion control, drainage, loading and waterproofing remain project-specific design checks.

Primary references:

- [Hong Kong Greening — Skyrise Greenery](https://www.greening.gov.hk/en/greening-landscape/right-plant-right-place/skyrise-greenery/index.html): extensive versus intensive roof planting and vertical greening.
- [ZinCo Green Roof Basics](https://zinco-greenroof.com/sites/default/files/2023-02/ZinCo_Green_Roof_Basics.pdf): flat, gable, butterfly, mono-pitch, saw-tooth and barrel roof examples; shape alone does not determine suitability.
- [ZinCo Pitched Green Roofs](https://zinco-greenroof.co.uk/systems/pitched_green_roof): special build-ups, transfer of shear forces, erosion protection and waterproofing for pitched roofs.
- [ZinCo Pitched Roof Planning Guide](https://zinco-greenroof.co.uk/sites/default/files/2025-02/Zinco_Pitched_Green_Roofs.pdf): bespoke curved-roof examples, including VanDusen Botanical Gardens.
